# Roadmap

## Now: 0.1.0 on the Force
The whole instrument, tested in one go. **To check:** it loads as an instrument and plays from pads and sequences;
Mono legato / retrigger and Poly; the Interzone pages' sliders, knobs and switches by touch and Q-Link, values above
the sliders; CV IN sources and amounts; the LFO, TIDAL / RANDOM and SEQ pages on the MPC tempo (play and stop); a
gate sequencer playing the voice with no key down; Ext Osc through the Ext slider; presets save and load; projects
save and reload; CPU (`tools/device_bench.sh`), to choose the default voice count. Then the GitHub release and the
catalog listing (mpc-vst-plugins).
